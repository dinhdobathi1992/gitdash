# GitDash v4.7.1 — release notes

Released 2026-10-01. **Product landing page** at `/welcome`, and an opt-in switch that shows it to
signed-out visitors at `/`. Helm chart 0.7.7 declares appVersion 4.7.1. No database migration. See
[`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md) for the itemised list.

## Why

The public site opened straight onto a sign-in form. A visitor who had never heard of GitDash saw a
token field before they saw what the product does.

## The landing page

`/welcome` is public and renders without the app's sidebar. In order:

- **Hero** — "Everything metrics, measured.", the one-line pitch, and an illustrated repositories view.
- **Product tour** — the 90-second intro video with nine chapters; each chapter seeks the same player.
  The video downloads only when a visitor presses play.
- **Deep dives** — DORA, workflow intelligence, team insights, cost and alerts, each with an illustration.
- **Everything included** — a 16-feature grid, and who it is built for.
- **Security & privacy**, **Get started** (Docker, Kubernetes, from source, Vercel — with a copy button),
  **Pricing** (Community free; Team; Enterprise marked *coming soon*) and an **FAQ**.

Product visuals are drawn from the app's own design tokens and labelled "Example"; none of the figures
are real data.

## Showing it at `/` — `GITDASH_LANDING_PAGE`

| `GITDASH_LANDING_PAGE` | Signed-out visitor opens `/` | Signed-in visitor opens `/` |
|---|---|---|
| unset / `false` (default) | `/login` (organization) or `/setup` (standalone), as before | Repositories |
| `true` | `/welcome` | Repositories |

The page's **Sign in** links go to `/login`; in standalone mode the server forwards that to `/setup`.
Deep links such as `/team` still go to sign-in. Helm: `config.landingPage: "true"`.

**Leave it off on a self-hosted instance** — it is meant for the public product site. `/welcome` itself is
reachable either way.

With it on, a session that expires sends the browser back to `/`, which now shows the landing page;
one click on Sign in continues.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. On Vercel, a new environment variable applies only after a redeploy.
